Services

PREPARE. STRATEGIZE.
CONNECT. NAVIGATE.

Four things an owner needs at different distances from the decision. You can take the first one and stop there — most owners should.

01

Prepare

Two years out, or ten

Most owners start the conversation two years too late. We look at your business the way a buyer will — recurring revenue, client concentration, margin, how much of it runs through you — and tell you what we see. You get a written picture of where you stand and what to work on, whether that day is next year or in ten.

02

Strategize

Before the decision is made

Selling is one option. Merging, recapitalising, buying someone else, or holding and getting stronger are others. We work through what each one does to your money, your people and your clients, so the decision is yours and it is made with the numbers in front of you.

03

Connect

Once you have decided

The right buyer is not the highest number on a page. It is the one whose plan survives contact with your people and your clients. Having sat on both sides, we know what each buyer actually does after closing — and we bring you the ones that fit.

04

Navigate

From signed engagement to close

A defined 150-day process: preparation, marketing materials including a custom-produced business video, a competitive process, diligence, and close. One person who has been the seller and the buyer, in the room the whole way.
