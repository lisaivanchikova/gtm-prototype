Where the numbers come from.
So you can argue with them.

A valuation tool that will not show its working is an advertisement. Here is the working.

Cost to replace you

Defaults to $105,770, the US median annual wage for General and Operations Managers (SOC 11-1021), Bureau of Labor Statistics, OEWS May 2025. It is a field, not a constant: change it to your market.

The multiple ladder

Set by adjusted earnings, then moved by the six factors. This is our model, not a published market survey, and it is deliberately conservative at the small end.

The range

The multiple plus or minus 0.6 turns. A range is where a conversation starts, not where it ends.

What it is not

Not an offer, not an appraisal, not a listing. Arithmetic on figures you typed yourself.
