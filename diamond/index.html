Where the numbers come from

So you can argue with them.

A valuation tool that will not show its working is an advertisement. Here is the working.

  • Cost to replace you — defaults to $105,770, the US median annual wage for General and Operations Managers (SOC 11-1021), Bureau of Labor Statistics, OEWS May 2025. It is a field, not a constant: change it to your market.
  • The multiple ladder — set by adjusted earnings, then moved by the six factors on the right. This is our model, not a published market survey, and it is deliberately conservative at the small end.
  • The range — the multiple plus or minus 0.6 turns. A range is where a conversation starts, not where it ends.
  • What it is not — not an offer, not an appraisal, not a listing. Arithmetic on figures you typed yourself.
