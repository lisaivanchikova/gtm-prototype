Where the numbers come from

Every figure here was typed in by an MSP owner finishing the review — nine questions, four minutes, no call. Nothing is sourced from a vendor survey, a database or an estimate.

Nothing published identifies anyone. What leaves the review page is the state, a revenue band and six scores. A name reaches Neil only if the owner asks for their own report or books a call.

A row is printed only once at least thirty owners have answered it that quarter. Anything thinner stays out of the report.
