How the number is made

  • ✓Disclosed prices from the deal index Only deals where a price was published. That is a small share of all deals, which is why a second source is needed.
  • ✓Figures owners submit to the E2E Score Revenue, contracted share, margin, concentration. Anonymous in the aggregate, never published per company.
  • ✓Minimum thirty answers before a band is printed Below that the band is withheld rather than estimated.
  • ≈Ranges, not point values A single number would be quoted back as an entitlement in every negotiation in the channel. Bands are honest and still usable.
