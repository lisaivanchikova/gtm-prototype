How the dates are checked

  • ✓Confirmed The organizer’s own page carries the date, and that page has been updated recently.
  • ?Derived Something had to be worked out — most often a year the organizer did not print. Shown, but marked, and queued for a call to the organizer.
  • !Held back The sources disagree, or a future date sits on a page nobody has touched in over a year. Not published until a human confirms it.

Three traps this exists to avoid: search engines return last year’s dates as current; events that have closed keep their landing pages live and ranking; and organizers print days without a year. All three were hit while building this calendar.
